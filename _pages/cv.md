***Rochester Institute of Technology / Independent,*** *Rochester, NY and Providence, RI*

* Developed Aethic Reasoning, a framework in which determinacy is indexed to observer-relative partial information states rather than treated as a monadic property of completed facts.  
* Developed the framework's state-relative treatment of temporal and modal openness, including the idea that an unlearned past can remain unsettled relative to a later information state without requiring backward dynamics.  
* Formulated the two-quantifier pruning rule over a reflexive-transitive descendant relation, used to classify states by whether their continuations can avoid contradiction.  
* Current work develops this base into a counterfactual semantics, an information-state algebra, and a finite path-kernel re-axiomatization of quantum measurement; completeness and the physical invalidity bridge remain open.

**7/2018 - present &emsp;Independent Research: Nexic & Target-Conditioned Reasoning**  
***Massachusetts / Providence, RI***

* Originated the **Accordance Principle**, a Bayesian typicality constraint relating the prior improbability of realized attributes to their relevance under observer- or target-conditioned sampling.  
* Developed Nexic Reasoning as a calculus of typicality over partial information states, separating general probabilistic structure from empirical claims about particular natural histories.  
* Developed quantitative tail, multiplicity, and stockpile bounds for collections of rare realized attributes, with explicit assumptions governing independence and calibration.  
* Applied the framework to natural history and astrobiology, including conditional Rare Earth results, evolutionary bottlenecks, and prospective tests of coupled historical prerequisites.  
* Extended the same Bayesian structure to future-targeted and survival-conditioned problems, yielding Targeted Accordance and a separate stochastic survivorship program.
